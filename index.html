Curator's Note

What becomes of an idea that is never permitted to begin?

An artwork that cannot begin. A decision that cannot be made. An approval that remains permanently pending.

Pending Approval (2026) is a conceptual exhibition about the strange beauty of waiting for permission.

This exhibition is conceptual. No actual authorization committee has been established.
